How GestureAI Recognizes Gestures

Our system combines computer vision, hand landmark detection and machine learning to recognize gestures from a live camera feed.

01

Camera Input

The webcam continuously captures frames of your hand and sends them to the recognition pipeline.

02

Hand Detection

MediaPipe detects the hand and identifies 21 important hand landmarks.

03

Feature Extraction

Landmark coordinates are converted into numerical features that can be understood by the ML model.

04

ML Classification

The trained Random Forest model analyzes the extracted features and predicts the gesture.

05

Live Result

The recognized gesture is displayed instantly on the live recognition dashboard.

06

Continuous Tracking

The system keeps processing incoming frames to provide a smooth real-time experience.
